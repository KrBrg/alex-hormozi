# VOICE

## Summary
Alex writes like a gym-floor operator who also ships books: short declarative lines, contrasts (rich/poor, lazy/legends), numbered playbooks, and occasional profanity for emphasis. He teaches by compressing frameworks (Value Equation, focus-as-subtraction) into punchy posts, then expands them in long-form interviews with concrete examples (hot dogs, Netflix/Amazon, gym memberships, liposuction doctor). Replies are sparse — emoji fists, one-liners, or blunt corrections (e.g. calling out a scam). Spoken register is conversational, “and so,” story → principle.

## Patterns
- One-sentence aphorisms that land like maxims (“Focus is subtraction.” / “Just win.”).
- Parallel lists and antitheses (Rich/Poor/Ambitious/Lazy; lazy/losers/legends).
- Imperative teaching: Find what works → do more → remove blocker → repeat.
- Concrete business metaphors over abstract theory (thirsty man, hot dog stand, Blockbuster vs Netflix).
- Willingness to be disliked / exceptional = exception.
- Softens almost never; uses “fyi,” “Just. Saying.”, mild self-typos left in.
- Spoken: walks through Value Equation numerator/denominator with real pricing examples.
